The brief.

This project was undertaken for one of India's leading investment firms struggling with high drop-off rates during new employee onboarding, directly affecting their hiring outcomes.

The objective was to revamp the employee onboarding portal to better cater to a growing workforce of millennials and Gen Z professionals. Within the constraints of a low-code development platform, the existing system was transformed into a more engaging, intuitive, and user-friendly experience.

What we solved for.

Retention gap Younger prospective hires were dropping off before Day One.
Lack of personalisation The onboarding experience lacked tailored content based on role, department, or individual preferences.
Inability to connect with the audience The platform's tone and visuals failed to resonate with millennial and Gen-Z users.
Outdated process The flow was rigid and inefficient, lacking modern UX patterns and mobile responsiveness.
Poor first impressions The interface did not reflect the company's culture or create a welcoming first experience.

Approach.

To understand the needs and expectations of our users, I began by benchmarking onboarding best practice from industry research (SHRM, HBR, TechCrunch, Forbes, and Gallup). These references helped me identify best practices and the gaps in the existing system.

In parallel, I ran in-depth interviews with recent hires and HR professionals within the company to uncover firsthand pain points, emotional responses, and unmet needs during onboarding. This dual approach, competitive analysis and qualitative research, gave me a well-rounded foundation for designing a more relevant and engaging experience.

Research findings.

User goals
Clarity: Understand role expectations, timelines, and responsibilities from the start Efficiency: Complete the onboarding process quickly Structure: A clear, guided path that shows progress and next steps
Motivations
Confidence: Feel prepared and capable before Day 1 Belonging: Develop a connection with the company's culture early Engagement: Stay interested and involved throughout
Behavioural patterns
Skimming: Users skimmed long text and ignored non-essential details Avoiding redundancy: Repetitive forms or unclear steps were often skipped
Pain points
Drop-off triggers: Confusing flows and lack of feedback led to incomplete onboarding Disconnection: The generic experience made users feel detached Anxiety: Uncertainty about what to expect caused hesitation

Designing within low-code constraints.

The platform was built on a low-code development environment, which introduced design limitations. I treated them as focus rather than friction.

Layout flexibility Limited flexibility in layout structures and grid customisation.
Interactions No support for advanced interactions or dynamic micro-animations.
Visual control Restricted styling beyond a set of predefined components.

These constraints helped us focus on clarity, structure, and user flow, making sure the experience stayed intuitive despite the technical limits.

Module 1

Creating clarity & confidence from Day One.

To address user pain points and build trust early in the journey, I designed an onboarding experience that:

Established a clear, structured flow to guide users and set the right expectations from the start. Built in accessible support touchpoints, making it easy for users to reach out for help when needed.

Improving task management & progress tracking.

Introduced visual timelines and highlighted key tasks to give users a clear sense of structure and reduce ambiguity. Added micro-interactions and visual feedback to make the experience more engaging.

Providing a human-centred support system.

Enabled users to connect with team members or managers early on, fostering a sense of belonging. Designed personalised, conversational interfaces to make onboarding feel more approachable and human.
Module 2

Streamlining the experience to reduce effort & uncertainty.

To simplify the onboarding process and reduce user drop-offs, I made the following changes:

Removed repetitive steps and made the total number of steps visible to set clear expectations. Enabled auto-fill from uploaded documents to reduce manual effort and speed up completion. Added visual cues and colour indicators to help users track progress effortlessly.

Impact & outcomes.

9.1 /10 Onboarding experience score
−80% Employee queries
+17% Onboarding success (YoY)

Reflecting high user satisfaction, improved clarity and usability, and a measurable lift in hiring outcomes year-over-year.
