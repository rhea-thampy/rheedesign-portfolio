Brands I've worked with

I like the products nobody volunteers to design. Payroll. Claims. Onboarding. The screens people have to use on a bad Tuesday.

I studied Industrial Design at the Florence Design Academy, which is a roundabout way of saying I was taught to care how a thing behaves before I care how it looks. Six years later that's still the job: sit with the operations team, find the step everyone quietly works around, and design the version they don't have to work around.

Based in Kochi, working across India, the UAE and the US. Off the clock I'm usually making something: clay, crochet, a long cooking project or a cuisine I haven't tried yet. There's also an ongoing argument with my sketchbook, and a running list of places I want to travel to next.
